# Story draft: The Long Winter

*Claude's draft, 2026-11-13, for the user to edit. Nothing here is decided except the sub-stage
counts (5 / 6 / 8 / 10 / 10). The design doc stays the source of truth for rules.*

## How the story is told

- **Text only.** Each stage has a short intro (shown the first time it opens) and an ending (after
  its boss falls). Each sub-stage has a title and one or two lines on its card.
- **Tone (decided 2026-11-13): light and funny, like an anime.** Each sub-stage is an episode. A
  small named cast with one strong trait each, running gags that pay off later, a loud rival who
  keeps coming back and ends up on our side, a mid-story reveal that changes who the enemy is, and
  a finale where everyone stands together.
- *Suggested for later:* show the lines as a speech bubble beside the speaker's sprite (the 4x
  portraits already exist), visual-novel style, before a run starts.
- **Why every run is a fence defense:** the player is the Commander of an expedition. Wherever the
  expedition stops, it builds a palisade camp, and the horde comes to it.
- **The "New to play" column** is the gameplay reason each sub-stage exists. These are first
  ideas for the wave tables, not built.

## The story in five sentences

Goblins raid a quiet frontier village, far more of them than ever before. The Commander drives
them off and follows their trail north, picking up allies on the way: the dwarves of Ironhold,
then the Beastfolk of the hills. At the Goblin Keep the truth comes out: the goblins aren't
conquering anything, they are running away. A Frost Wyrm has woken in the far north, and its
winter is pushing every creature south. The expedition marches into the snow to end it.

## Recurring characters

All are existing heroes and bosses, so no new art is needed. **Enemies are called by their title
(user, 2026-11-13: "the Goblin King" is the name). The heroes' names are Claude's
suggestions (the user asked for names, 2026-11-13), placeholders to change freely.**

| Name | Who | Trait | Running gag |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Aldric** | Shield Knight, captain of the village guard | The serious one. Does everything by the rulebook | Never gets to finish a cup of tea |
| **Lira** | Leaf Archer, a Sylvan scout | The energetic one. Acts first, thinks on the way | Touches things she was just told not to touch |
| **Kitsu** | Fox Merchant, joins "for the business opportunities" | The schemer with a soft heart | Tries to sell something in every crisis |
| **Greta** | Tinker of Ironhold (from stage 2) | The obsessed engineer | Talks to the fence, and has named it |
| **Olwen** | Owl Sage (from stage 3) | The wise one who works out the mystery | Falls asleep in the middle of explaining it |
| **Bram** | Bear Warden (from stage 3) | The gentle giant | Says one word where others say twenty |
| **Tunn** | Badger Digger (from stage 3) | The grumpy expert on holes | "That's not one of mine" |
| **The Goblin King** | Boss | The rival. Loud, proud, always loses, always returns | Declares the Commander an "eternal rival" |
| **The Goblin Warlord** | Boss | Tired, honourable, the real leader | The only goblin who sighs |
| **The Briar Witch** | Boss | Proud and misunderstood | Insists the briars are "a garden" |

The player is **the Commander** and never speaks. The Frost Wyrm has no name anyone remembers.

## Stage 1: Greenwood Road (5 sub-stages)

**Intro:** Harvest week in Greenwood. The road north has been quiet for years. This morning the
Leaf Archer came running down it. "Goblins. A lot of goblins. And they look... in a hurry."

| # | Title | Card text | New to play |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1-1 | Smoke on the Road | The first raiders reach the fence before breakfast. Aldric has not had tea. Aldric is not pleased. | Imps only, in swarms. Teaches buying, placing and the fence |
| 1-2 | Quick Feet | Scamps. They don't stop to fight, they don't stop to talk, they don't stop at all. | Goblin Scamps, which run past melee heroes |
| 1-3 | The Big Ones | The ground shakes a little. Kitsu raises prices a little. | Orc Bruisers, which stop to fight heroes outside the fence |
| 1-4 | The Warchief's Horn | A horn in the trees, and a green glow behind it. Someone is giving the orders. | Bog Shamans (damage aura); ends on the Orc Warchief |
| 1-5 | The Goblin King | "I am the Goblin King, and this village is my new summer palace!" He has a crown, a throne carried by six Scamps, and no plan to go back north. | The full stage; boss: Goblin King |

**Ending:** The Goblin King runs, shouting over his shoulder: "This isn't over, Commander! From today
you are my eternal rival!" He leaves his crown in the mud. It is cold to the touch, rimmed with
frost in the middle of harvest. That night a dwarf rides in on a very tired pony: Iron Pass is
under siege, and the hold asks for help. *(Ironhold joins.)*

## Stage 2: Iron Pass (6 sub-stages)

**Intro:** The road climbs into grey stone. The dwarves have held Iron Pass for three hundred
years and would like everyone to know it. Greta meets the expedition at the first gate,
already measuring the fence.

| # | Title | Card text | New to play |
|---|---|---|---|
| 2-1 | The Gate Road | Goblins on the mountain road, and behind them, something that clanks. | Familiar goblins; the first few Shieldbearers |
| 2-2 | Shield Wall | "They've stolen our shields," says Greta. "Good shields, too. That's the problem." | Shieldbearers in numbers (armor; poison ignores it) |
| 2-3 | Stones from Above | The fence takes a hit before any enemy is near it. Then another. Look up. | Rock Slingers, which hit the fence from range |
| 2-4 | The Sergeant | One of them has learned to shout "FORM UP". It is working. | Ends on the Iron Sergeant |
| 2-5 | The Broken Bridge | Slingers on the far side, shields in front, and only one way across. | Slingers behind a shield wall |
| 2-6 | The Mountain Troll | It woke up cold and hungry, and the pass was in its way. | The full stage; boss: Mountain Troll |

**Ending:** The Troll's cave is full of ice. "In summer?" says Greta. A hawk drops out of
the sky with a message tied to its leg: the Wild Hills are closing over with thorns, and the
Beastfolk can't hold them alone. *(Beastfolk join.)*

## Stage 3: Wild Hills (8 sub-stages)

**Intro:** The hills should be green and loud. They are green and quiet, and the paths Bram
grew up on end in walls of briar. Olwen has been counting the birds flying south.
There are too many.

| # | Title | Card text | New to play |
|---|---|---|---|
| 3-1 | Thorns on the Trail | The raiders here don't stay down. Something small and mossy keeps patching them up. | The first Moss Menders, which heal other enemies |
| 3-2 | Something Under the Grass | Tunn puts an ear to the ground. "That's not one of mine." | Tunnel Grubs, which travel underground |
| 3-3 | The Mending Circle | Three Menders, standing close, each healing the other two. Rude. | Several Menders at once: kill order matters |
| 3-4 | Molehills | So many mounds that Kitsu tries to sell them as real estate. | Grubs in numbers, surfacing near the fence |
| 3-5 | The Elder Mender | Old, slow, and very good at its job. Everything near it gets better. | Ends on the Elder Mender |
| 3-6 | Shields in the Briars | The clanking ones followed us from the pass. They have found friends. | Iron Pass enemies return, with Menders behind them |
| 3-7 | The Witch's Garden | Every thorn here points north, like a fence. Olwen writes that down. | Shamans, Menders and Grubs together |
| 3-8 | The Briar Witch | "It's a garden," says the Briar Witch. "I didn't grow it to keep you out. But you're trampling it now." | The full stage; boss: Briar Witch |

**Ending:** The briars fall back. Olwen reads the notes aloud: the thorns were a wall
against the north wind, the goblins came through anyway, and all of them were heading the same
way. South. Away. "We should ask the goblins what they're running from." The Keep is two days'
march.

## Stage 4: Goblin Keep (10 sub-stages)

**Intro:** The Goblin Keep is big, ugly and badly built, and every goblin for a hundred miles is
packed inside it. They are not coming out to talk. The expedition builds its camp at the ditch.

| # | Title | Card text | New to play |
|---|---|---|---|
| 4-1 | The Outer Ditch | The welcome party. Mostly Imps, mostly angry. | A big mixed goblin wave, a warm-up with everything from stage 1 |
| 4-2 | Short Fuses | A goblin runs at the fence carrying a barrel. It is smiling. That's the worrying part. | Goblin Sappers, which blow up at the fence |
| 4-3 | The Powder Store | Greta finds where the barrels come from. So do forty Sappers. | Sappers in numbers |
| 4-4 | Wall Guards | Stolen shields on the walls, stolen stones on the towers. | Shieldbearers and Rock Slingers together |
| 4-5 | The King Returns | "Eternal rival! We meet again!" The Goblin King has a new crown. It is a cooking pot. Nobody mentions it. | Ends on the Goblin King as mini-boss |
| 4-6 | The Kitchens | The Keep feeds ten thousand goblins. The kitchen staff have ladles and strong feelings. | Imp swarms with Shamans |
| 4-7 | The Tunnels | Under the Keep, the walls are wet, the Grubs are fat, and the fuses are lit. | Grubs and Sappers together |
| 4-8 | The Sick Ward | Menders by the dozen. The Keep has been patching up its wounded for weeks. | Menders behind Orc Bruisers |
| 4-9 | The Throne Room Doors | The Warchief and the Sergeant hold the doors. It is the last door. | Everything so far, with two mini-bosses as elites |
| 4-10 | The Goblin Warlord | The one the Goblin King answers to. Tired eyes, heavy armor, and a hall full of goblins with nowhere else to go. | The full stage; boss: Goblin Warlord |

**Ending:** The Warlord drops the axe and sits down with a long sigh. The north half of the Keep is
frozen solid, and has been for a month. "It woke up," the Warlord says. "The Wyrm. We ran. Wouldn't
you?" Kitsu, for once, has nothing to sell. The expedition turns north. The Goblin King insists on
coming along, "to keep an eye on my eternal rival".

## Stage 5: Frozen Reach (10 sub-stages)

**Intro:** Past the Keep the snow starts, and doesn't stop. The lights in the storm are not
lanterns. Aldric has brought tea this time, and it has frozen in the cup.

| # | Title | Card text | New to play |
|---|---|---|---|
| 5-1 | First Snow | Everything that didn't run south is still here, and it is hungry. | Familiar enemies in the snow, a warm-up |
| 5-2 | Cold Lights | Pretty blue lights drift toward the camp. Lira reaches out. Don't. | Frost Wisps, which freeze a hero |
| 5-3 | Walking Ice | It shrugs off the first hit, and the second. Keep hitting. Bits are falling off. | Ice Golems, whose armor sheds with each hit |
| 5-4 | Whiteout | Wisps in front, Golems behind, and you can't see either until they're close. | Wisps and Golems together |
| 5-5 | The Glacier Golem | The hill moved. Hills shouldn't move. | Ends on the Glacier Golem |
| 5-6 | The Frozen Camp | Someone built a fence here before us. Greta says it was good work. | Shieldbearers and Slingers with Wisps |
| 5-7 | Under the Ice | Tunn listens to the ice. "Still not mine." | Grubs and Sappers under Golems |
| 5-8 | The Wyrm's Shadow | It passes over the camp once, slowly, and the fire goes out. | A long wave with every healer and summoner |
| 5-9 | The Last Fire | One more night to hold. Everyone who can stand is on the wall. Even the Goblin King, cooking pot and all. "Nobody beats my rival but me!" | Everything so far, the hardest wave tables in the game |
| 5-10 | The Frost Wyrm | Old as the mountains and just as cold. It isn't angry. It is only winter, and it is here. | The full stage; boss: Frost Wyrm |

**Ending:** The Wyrm folds its wings and goes back under the ice to sleep. By morning the snow is
melting. In Greenwood they finish the harvest three weeks late; the goblins help, badly, and Kitsu
sells them hats. Aldric finally finishes a cup of tea. Olwen still has one question nobody can answer: what woke
it up? *(A hook for later stages.)*
