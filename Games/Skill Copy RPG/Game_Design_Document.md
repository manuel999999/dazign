## Logline
*Draft:* Defeat monsters to steal their skills and traits, then grow into an overpowered hero built from everything you've ever fought.

## Scope Notes
- Content-heavy: every monster needs a copyable skill or trait, so the monster roster and the skill roster grow together.
- **Suggested vertical slice:** 8-10 monsters each with a copyable skill/trait, loadout system, one area.
- May share an RPG core with Auto RPG and the MU-like RPG.
