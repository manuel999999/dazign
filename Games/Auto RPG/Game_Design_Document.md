## Core Gameplay Loop
1. The character is placed on a stage map and auto-fights monsters.
2. Monsters drop loot. The character gets stronger.
3. Reaching a threshold unlocks the next stage.
4. The player decides whether to move up. A higher stage means stronger monsters and better loot, but the character may not survive there yet.
5. If the character struggles, drop back, farm and gear up, then push again.
